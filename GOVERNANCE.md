# Governance

pipeloom has a single maintainer, Emanuel Luis (@emanuel-luis), who makes the final call on
scope, design and releases. Significant decisions are written down as ADRs in `docs/adr/` so
the reasoning stays visible.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who land several substantial changes, review other people's work and follow the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) can be invited as maintainers. The maintainer sends the
invitation and updates `CODEOWNERS` and this file. A maintainer who is inactive for a long
period may step down to emeritus.

## Changing this model

Open an ADR. The model may move to a maintainer group as the project grows.
